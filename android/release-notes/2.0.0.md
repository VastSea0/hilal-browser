Hilal Browser for Android 2.0 is a new app, rewritten from scratch on Material 3 Expressive.

### Gestures on the address bar

- Tap to type an address or search; suggestions appear right above your thumb.
- Swipe left or right to switch tabs. The page follows your finger.
- Swipe up for the tab overview.
- Long press to paste and go, copy, or share.
- The bar hides while you read and comes back when you scroll up.

### New

- History, bookmarks and open tabs are saved and restored.
- Downloads keep the site's login and appear in your Downloads folder.
- Sites can ask for location, camera, microphone and notifications, and you decide each time.
- Add-ons ask before they get new permissions. uBlock Origin is included.
- Website dialogs, sign-in prompts, dropdowns and file uploads work.
- Light, dark and wallpaper colors; optional dark websites.
- Vibration only when a swipe is far enough to act, and it can be turned off.

### Privacy

Search suggestions are never requested from private tabs. The anonymous daily usage ping is
unchanged and can be turned off in Settings → Privacy.
