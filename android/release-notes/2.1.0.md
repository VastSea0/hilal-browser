Hilal Browser for Android 2.1 introduces a customizable new tab page and enhanced Liquid Glass styling.

### Customizable New Tab Page

- Choose from dynamic ambient glow presets (Aurora, Dusk, Midnight, Sunset, Emerald, Cotton Candy) or pick your own custom wallpaper with adjustable dimming and blur.
- Flexible header options: Logo with custom greeting, live clock and date, minimal logo, or hidden.
- Pinned shortcuts management: add, edit, reorder, or remove shortcuts, with expressively shaped monograms.
- Tile customization: adjust tile sizes (Compact, Medium, Large) and shape styles (Squircle, Rounded, Continuous, Organic, Expressive).
- Toggleable sections for on-page search, quick access chips, pinned shortcuts, frequent sites, bookmarks, and update notices.

### Liquid Glass

- Authentic backdrop sampling with real-time lens refraction, frosted dynamic blur, and specular rim highlights.
- Redesigned bottom sheet customize drawer that refracts the page content smoothly.
- Traveling liquid glass selection pill on segmented controls.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Resolved cyclic backdrop rendering and drawer gesture conflict.
- Smoother transitions and scrolling across bottom sheets.
