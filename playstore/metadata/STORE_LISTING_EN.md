## Full Description
> Limit: Maximum 4000 characters.

```text
Hilal Browser is a modern, privacy-first Android web browser built on independent web standards and crafted with Google's Material 3 Expressive design language.

Engineered around the Mozilla GeckoView engine as a principled alternative to Chromium-based dominance, Hilal provides a blazing-fast browsing experience while strictly protecting your personal data from third-party trackers, advertisers, and surveillance networks.

KEY FEATURES

🌙 Independent Mozilla GeckoView Engine
Hilal utilizes Mozilla's independent Gecko rendering engine. Enjoy full web standards compliance, modern CSS/JavaScript capabilities, hardware-accelerated rendering, and an open, decentralized web ecosystem.

🛡️ Uncompromising Privacy & Tracker Protection
- Strict Tracker Blocking: Automatically halts intrusive third-party ads, analytics, and fingerprinting scripts before they load.
- WebRTC & Leak Prevention: Prevents IP and local network leaks across websites.
- Zero Telemetry: Hilal does not track you, does not harvest your browsing habits, and never uploads your personal data to remote servers.
- One-Tap Wipe: Instantly purge your history, cache, and cookies with a single touch whenever you need.

📁 Smart Tab Workspaces
Eliminate tab chaos forever! Hilal introduces intuitive Workspaces to organize your web browsing:
- Create isolated spaces for Work, Research, Shopping, and Personal browsing.
- Each workspace keeps its own tabs active and neatly grouped so you can maintain deep focus.
- Switch between spaces effortlessly via the ergonomic bottom action sheet.

⚡ Hilal Bangs: Instant Direct Search
Skip the search middleman! Type direct shortcuts into the Omnibox to jump directly to search results:
- !g [query] -> Search directly on Google
- !yt [query] -> Search directly on YouTube
- !w [query] -> Search directly on Wikipedia
- !ddg [query] -> Search directly on DuckDuckGo
- Create and customize your own custom bangs anytime within Settings.

🎨 Material 3 Expressive Design & Ergonomics
- Fluid & Modern: Embraces Android's latest Material 3 Expressive design system with smooth animations and dynamic theming.
- Floating or Docked Toolbars: Customize your URL bar and bottom toolbar style to float elegantly or stay docked.
- Auto-Hide on Scroll: Bars smoothly retract as you scroll down web pages, maximizing your screen real estate.
- Dark Mode for Websites: Automatically renders supported websites in a restful dark theme.
- True One-Handed Usability: Essential controls, tab switcher, and options are ergonomically located at the bottom of the screen.

🔒 Secure Private Tabs
Open private tabs that store cookies and session state strictly in temporary memory. When you close your private tabs, all session data vanishes instantly.

OPEN SOURCE & TRANSPARENT
Hilal Browser is built in the open with a commitment to user sovereignty, security, and digital privacy.

Download Hilal Browser today and experience a faster, cleaner, and truly private web!
```
