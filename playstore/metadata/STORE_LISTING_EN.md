## Full Description
> Limit: Maximum 4000 characters.

```text
Hilal is a free and open source Android browser built on Mozilla's GeckoView engine, the same engine as Firefox. It comes with uBlock Origin and an address bar you use with your thumb.

ADDRESS BAR GESTURES
- Tap to type an address or search. Suggestions appear just above your thumb.
- Swipe left or right to switch tabs.
- Swipe up to see all your tabs.
- Long press to paste and go, copy the link, or share it.
- The bar hides while you read and comes back when you scroll up.

PRIVACY
- uBlock Origin is included and turned on.
- Tracking protection blocks known trackers, cryptominers, and fingerprinting scripts, and keeps third-party cookies separate for each site.
- Private tabs forget their history, cookies, and site data when you close them. Search suggestions are never requested from private tabs.
- Sites must ask before using your location, camera, or microphone, and you decide each time.
- Hilal sends an anonymous usage count once a day (app version, Android version, language, search engine, and a rough tab count, with a random ID). No browsing data is sent, and you can turn it off in Settings.

SEARCH SHORTCUTS
Start a search with a shortcut to go straight to a site's results, for example !w for Wikipedia, !yt for YouTube, or !gh for GitHub. Choose DuckDuckGo, Google, Brave, Bing, Ecosia, Startpage, or Yandex as your search engine.

EVERYTHING ELSE
- History, bookmarks, and open tabs are saved and restored.
- Downloads keep the site's sign-in and go to your Downloads folder.
- Find in page, add-ons from addons.mozilla.org, and website sign-in prompts.
- Light, dark, and wallpaper colors, with optional dark websites.

Hilal has no ads and does not sell data. The source code is on GitHub: github.com/VastSea0/hilal-browser
```
